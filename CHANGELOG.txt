ROUND BOOK - RELEASE NOTES
==========================

Newest first. This file is not part of the app and is not loaded by it; it ships alongside the code files.
To add a release: raise APP_VERSION in js/01-data.js and CACHE_NAME in sw.js to the same number, then put a new entry at the top of this list.

Version 3.17
  - Customer info box (press and hold a card): a camera button at the top opens the camera straight away, and the
    photo is saved to that customer. You stay in the info box afterwards. (Photos from the library are still added
    from Open full details > Photos)

Version 3.16
  - The yellow number for quotes needing attention now sits on the Quotes sub tab (Sales > Quotes) instead of on the
    Sales tab
  - Today on iPad and computer: the big card is on the left with the Text before visit and Mileage tiles (and Jobs
    today) underneath it. The right-hand column is the Owing tile with the list of customers who owe underneath,
    most overdue first, as the same swipeable cards as Rounds > Owed. Phones are unchanged

Version 3.15
  - Search by date: a 📅 button beside the search box opens the calendar. Pick a day and Search lists everything the
    app has recorded on it: customers cleaned (and what was charged), payments received, one-off jobs, messages
    sent, quotes dated or changed, photos added, price changes, mileage and the day's note, with a total at the top.
    Tap any row to open it. You can also type a date (14/10, 14/10/26, 14 oct, today, yesterday)

Version 3.14
  - iPad and computer: on the Work and Sales tabs the main tab bar (which sits at the top there) no longer has
    rounded lower corners, so it joins the sub tab bar cleanly. (3.13 had only squared the phone header)

Version 3.13
  - Schedule: Note and Days off now sit behind the ⋯ More… button with Push back, Multi, Split and ＋ Job, leaving
    the top row for Today, zoom and Undo
  - Work and Sales: the header's rounded lower corners are removed on those tabs, so it sits flush against the sub
    tab bar

Version 3.12
  - The Work (Rounds / Jobs) and Sales (Quotes / Marketing) sub tabs now use the same see-through glass bar as the
    main tabs, with the active one as a lifted glass pill
  - The Schedule now has the same glass look: translucent panes for the title bar, the tool buttons (now rounded
    capsules), the day names and the day boxes, and a glass pop-up
  - The Glass transparency slider now also changes the header, the main tab bar and the sub tabs (before, those
    stayed at one fixed strength)

Version 3.11
  - Search is now forgiving. A misspelled customer, address or round name still finds results (one slip in a
    short word, two in a long one: swapped, missing, extra or wrong letters), and the closest matches are listed
    first. Road shorthand is understood both ways (Rd and Road, St and Street, Ave, Cres, Dr, Ln and so on), capitals,
    accents and apostrophes are ignored, and several words can be typed in any order. Phone numbers can be typed
    with or without spaces. When nothing matches exactly, a line says so and shows the closest. Phone numbers,
    emails and notes still need to match exactly

Version 3.10
  - Today: the Start, Stop and round buttons are now solid colours (Stop is solid red, and a switched-off button is
    solid grey instead of faded)
  - Settings: a new Glass transparency slider, from Clear to Solid with Standard in the middle, that changes how
    see-through the header, panels, cards, tiles, pop-ups and the Today big card are, as you drag it. Reset to
    standard puts it back. It is kept on each device. (The High contrast scheme has no glass, so it is unaffected)

Version 3.09
  - The glass effect is slightly more see-through in every colour scheme, light and dark
  - Today: while a Start run is going, the big card no longer has a solid background. It is now see-through navy
    glass with the list scrolling underneath it, and the strip behind it is clear

Version 3.08
  - Message other device (⋯ menu): type a short note and it appears as a card at the top of Today on your other
    device (and stays pinned above the big card during a Start run) until you tap Got it. Dismissing it tells the
    sender it has been read. It travels with live sync, encrypted like the rest of your data, and arrives within about
    a minute while the other device has the app open. Read messages tidy themselves away after three days

Version 3.07
  - Today: on a customer who is already cleaned today, swiping right (even all the way across) no longer records a
    payment; it just says they are already cleaned. To record the payment use a left swipe or the Mark paid button
    (the card's right-swipe label also reads Already cleaned instead of Cleaned + Paid)

Version 3.06
  - Today: pressing Start now shrinks the big card to just the totals and the progress bar (the round buttons are
    hidden while the list is running; Stop brings them back)
  - A customer can now only be marked cleaned once a day. Marking them cleaned again the same day (swipe, button or
    adding a date by hand in their history) is refused with a message

Version 3.05
  - More > Check for updates: looks for a newer version on demand and offers it with the usual Update banner. If you
    are already up to date it says so, and offers to clear the app's stored program files and download them fresh
    (your customers and data are not touched) for the rare time the app seems stuck on an old version

Version 3.04
  - Today: while a Start run is going, the day's note now stays pinned above the big card as you scroll down the
    list (a very long note scrolls inside its own box)

Version 3.03
  - Today: a note left on today in the Schedule now always sits above the big card, including while a Start run is
    going (it used to move down into the list)

Version 3.02
  - Today: on the Start list, each customer's card now shows how much they owe (red Owes badge) or have in credit
    (green Credit badge). Customers who are paid up show nothing extra

Version 3.01
  - Printed schedules (the Schedule tab's print and the Monthly Schedule report): long round names now wrap onto
    extra lines instead of being cut short
  - iPad and desktop: a report's preview now has its zoom buttons and its Print / Share PDF / Share doc buttons in a
    sidebar down the left, so the page gets the full height. Phones are unchanged

Version 3.00
  - Today: after you finish a customer on a Start run, the list now moves up half a tile further, so the one you
    just finished is half tucked under the big card and the next one has more room
  - Schedule: a print icon next to the help icon at the top right prints the calendar as you are viewing it (all the
    weeks shown, earlier weeks included, with any changes not yet committed, days off, notes, and cleans already done
    in green)
  - Customer cards: the call button now offers Call or Text when the number is a mobile (Text opens the usual message
    screen, text or WhatsApp as set for that customer). A landline still just calls

Version 2.99
  - Schedule: you can now look back at what was cleaned. Tap ◀ Show 4 earlier weeks above the calendar (◀ Earlier in
    the side panel on iPad and computer) as often as you like, up to two years. Past days show green ✓ tiles for each
    round cleaned and any one-off jobs done, with the number of customers and the money for the day; tap a tile for the
    list of who was cleaned and what they paid. They are a record only and can't be moved

Version 2.98
  - Today: the Start list now follows your visiting route (visit day, then the order set under Reorder), the same
    order a round's own Due list uses, instead of most-overdue-first
  - Today: Start and Stop are now switched off only on the wide two-column layout (a landscape iPad or a computer);
    an iPad held upright keeps them, as do phones

Version 2.97
  - Today: the Start and Stop buttons are switched off on iPad and desktop-sized screens (they remain on phones);
    the progress bar stays

Version 2.96
  - The user guide has been rewritten to be shorter and simpler, and its feedback section removed
  - The in-app version history has been removed; release notes now live in this file
    (CHANGELOG.txt), which ships alongside the code

Version 2.95
  - iPad and desktop: lighter, easier-to-read text. Bold weights step down one notch, text uses
    grayscale smoothing (the default on a Mac made it look thick), small grey labels have more
    contrast and the base size is a touch larger. Phones are unchanged

Version 2.94
  - iPad and desktop: the list on the left now scrolls in its own column with its own scrollbar at
    the column edge (it used to use the window scrollbar at the far side of the screen, beside the
    right-hand pane), and the list stays scrollable while something is open on the right

Version 2.93
  - Today: Start and Stop buttons under the big card. Start swaps the tiles for today's to-do list
    (same cards as Rounds > Due, plus today's jobs) with the big card pinned above it and the list
    scrolling underneath; finished customers stay in view and the list moves up from the second one
    on. A 0–100% progress bar sits at the bottom of the big card, and the date above it has gone (it
    is in the header)

Version 2.92
  - Day notes from the Schedule now print on the Monthly Schedule report (a long note is given in
    full under the calendar). On iPad and computer the two columns are switched off while the
    Schedule tab is showing, so nothing from the right-hand pane shows through

Version 2.91
  - New tab layout: Today, Schedule, Work (Rounds / Jobs) and Sales (Quotes / Marketing), with the
    sub tabs pinned under the top and remembering where you were. The Schedule is now its own tab
    and keeps your unsaved changes while you look at other tabs; Reject / Commit are smaller; you
    can leave a note on any day; and a new round can be given a day before its first clean

Version 2.90
  - Live sync: opening the app no longer asks for end-of-day mileage before your other device's
    answer has arrived, and a screen being open can no longer hold sync back while the top says
    Synced. The top now says Update waiting in that case and catches up the moment the screen closes

Version 2.89
  - Pop-ups with a box to type in (such as editing an amount in a history, or the Schedule's
    dialogs) now open at the top so the keyboard can't cover them; a photo's buttons stay pinned at
    the bottom while the photo scrolls underneath

Version 2.88
  - iPad and computer: the empty right-hand pane now shows a summary for the tab you're on; reports
    preview inside it; drag the divider to resize it; right-click menus on cards; hover highlights;
    and keyboard moves through the list (j / k, Enter, C, P)

Version 2.87

    who have no mobile number

Version 2.86

Version 2.85

    ?test to the web address)

Version 2.84
